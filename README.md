# Frames of Gita reference library

Reference collection for the Frames of Gita Instagram project, checked 27 October 2026

## New here?

Read [GETTING-STARTED.md](GETTING-STARTED.md) for how to make a video from scratch: tools, workflow, export settings and honest limits. Ready-to-adapt prompts live in [prompts/](prompts/) with sources in [prompts/SOURCES.md](prompts/SOURCES.md).

Study the storytelling and visual techniques, not a licence to reuse another creator's art, voice, music or footage publicly

## Start with the style that serves the idea

- **Clay-look stories** - warm faces, tactile props and miniature rooms for emotional parables
- **2D illustrated stories** - recurring characters, comparison panels and visual scoreboards for narrative or debate
- **3D ritual demonstrations** - camera paths, footprints and cutaways for something physical that needs explaining
- **Cultural reveals** - a familiar object becomes a doorway to an unfamiliar Indian connection
- **Talking-head explainers** - a direct misconception, concrete explanation and everyday analogy
- **Production craft** - asset quality, continuous movement, composition and narration-led planning

These are creative judgments, not evidence that one style will outperform another

## Strong examples to study

### Clay-look emotional storytelling

Account: https://www.instagram.com/clayrified_/reels/

- [King and ring](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DelBsHFMJxh/) - 66 seconds, user-shared reference
  - A strange commission establishes a question, the inscription stays hidden, then the same ring means something different in prosperity and defeat
  - Warm/cool lighting, miniature rooms, facial close-ups and a tiny physical object carry the feeling
  - Good fit for emotional parables, with restrained character design for serious scenes
  - The story is not established as a Gita story or quotation
  - Clay-look is verified visually, physical stop-motion and exact software are not established by the hashtags
- [Two monks and the woman](https://www.instagram.com/clayrified_/reel/Ddm7_DEMQwJ/) - 78 seconds, additional high-play account example
  - 36.9M displayed profile-grid plays at inspection, not a retention statistic
  - An outwardly finished incident stays alive in one character's mind, revealed through walking scenes, expressions and a final line
  - Modern everyday examples follow the parable
  - Source tradition and wording require independent checking before adaptation

### 2D storybook narrative and visual bookkeeping

Account: https://www.instagram.com/loreandproof/reels/

- [Thunder gods and serpents](https://www.instagram.com/reels/De2HDdEvUFe/) - 75 seconds, user-shared
  - 1.8M displayed profile-grid plays at inspection
  - Three parallel stories establish a pattern, then a question pivots into language evidence and a family-tree diagram
  - Flat outlined characters, layered landscapes, navy/gold cards and bold cream/yellow subtitles
  - Ending returns to the opening image with an emotional line
  - Creator cites Rigveda 1.32, Hesiod's Theogony 820-868, Prose Edda Gylfaginning 51 and Calvert Watkins, How to Kill a Dragon
  - Those citations and the common-ancestry hypothesis have not been independently verified here
- [Mahabharata and 18](https://www.instagram.com/loreandproof/reel/De2CSOuybFl/) - 75 seconds, additional account example
  - 642K displayed profile-grid plays
  - Repeated numbers build curiosity, icon multiplication explains the army quantities, then a partial correction separates arithmetic from literary interpretation
  - Useful structure: pattern, escalation, question, correction
  - Creator cites Ganguli's translation, not independently verified here
- [Arjun or Karna](https://www.instagram.com/loreandproof/reel/De68FtNxQAh/) - 79 seconds, additional account example
  - A running scoreboard lets the viewer follow a sequence of fights, then a four-slot loss checklist changes the perspective
  - Specific fight counts and the attributed Krishna line need source checks

The account displays an AI-content label, which does not establish a particular tool or workflow

### 3D rituals and architectural mechanisms

Account: https://www.instagram.com/therishikatha/reels/

- [Clockwise temple route](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DezesK9zUot/) - 54 seconds, user-shared
  - A lamp-lit temple, overhead camera and glowing footprints demonstrate a route
  - A second question halfway through introduces the water channel and explains why the depicted path stops
  - The animation teaches something physical, rather than decorating narration
  - Creator cites Shiva Rahasya 7.26.80, not independently verified here, and the universal wording about temple practice should not be repeated without checking scope

Account: https://www.instagram.com/undiscovered.bharat/reels/

- [Sunlight and zodiac pillars](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DemOuPaM-7R/) - 44 seconds, user-shared
  - 74.1K displayed profile-grid plays
  - An architectural roof cutaway reveals something ordinary exterior footage would hide
  - Moving shafts of sunlight, pillar labels and cream-background diagrams show the claimed mechanism
  - Claims about Sri Vidyashankara Temple's alignments and solstices require independent historical and astronomical checks
- [Snakes and Ladders](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Dey_O80MbTK/) - 59 seconds, user-shared
  - 1.4M displayed profile-grid plays
  - Familiar childhood game, unexpected cultural connection, board labels and moving counters, then a bigger meaning
  - Strong model for genuine cultural connections, not a reason to force a spiritual moral onto an unrelated fable
  - Claims about origins, board traditions and meanings need independent checking

### Scripture and philosophy as visual arguments

Account: https://www.instagram.com/thehindu.archive/reels/

- [Ancient texts and classics](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Den9-ijy7fl/) - user-shared

  - This clip's play count was not recovered, the reel's like count must not substitute for views
